Speed Page: set it up once

VJster plays a YouTube video here and the deck sets its speed live, 0.25x to 2x.

  1. In Stream Deck, open any VJster action's settings. Under OBS page, tick Keep the link on.
  2. Paste a YouTube link, for example youtube.com/watch?v=aqz-KE-bpKQ (Big Buck Bunny), then press Copy address.
  3. In OBS, open the Speed Page source's Properties, untick Local file, paste the address as the URL and press OK.
  4. Press the kit's VJster keys, or set a VJster action's Control to OBS page.

The address carries this computer's OBS page key: keep it out of screenshots and chat, and do not share or export this scene collection after pasting it. If OBS shows an error page, start Stream Deck and refresh the source.
